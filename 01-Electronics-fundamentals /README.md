Electronics Fundamentals

Overview

This section establishes the foundational knowledge required to understand, analyze, and build electronic circuits.

Topics

- Electrical charge
- Voltage
- Electric current
- Resistance
- Electrical power and energy
- Conductors, insulators, and semiconductors
- Basic circuit analysis
- Series and parallel circuits
- Ohm's law
- Kirchhoff's laws
- Electronic components and their functions
- Circuit measurement and troubleshooting

Learning Approach

Each topic is studied through conceptual explanations, relatable examples, calculations, and circuit simulations using available online tools.

Objective

Develop a solid understanding of fundamental electrical and electronic principles before progressing to advanced circuits, microcontrollers, embedded systems, robotics, and industrial automation.

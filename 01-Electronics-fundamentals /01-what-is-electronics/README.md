What Is Electronics?

Overview

Electronics is the field concerned with controlling electrical charge and signals to process information, make decisions, and perform useful functions.

Key Concepts

- Electronics uses components and circuits to control electrical behaviour.
- Sensors detect physical conditions and convert them into signals.
- Controllers interpret signals and determine appropriate actions.
- Electronic systems can control other devices, such as lamps, motors, and pumps.

Practical Example: Automatic Streetlight

An automatic streetlight uses a light sensor to detect ambient light levels. A controller interprets the sensor signal and switches the lamp on when darkness is detected.

System Components

- Input: Light sensor
- Processing: Controller
- Output: Lamp switching mechanism

Electronics vs. Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineering often focuses on electrical energy generation, distribution, conversion, and utilization. Electronics often focuses on controlling electrical signals and charge using electronic components and circuits. The fields overlap in many applications.

Key Takeaway

Electronics enables systems to sense conditions, process electrical signals, and control devices. It is a fundamental building block of embedded systems, robotics, and industrial automation.
